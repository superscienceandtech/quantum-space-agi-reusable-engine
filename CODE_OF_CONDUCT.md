# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for
everyone participating in this project, regardless of background or identity.
Project spaces include issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other
project-related communications.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- communicating with respect and consideration;
- welcoming constructive feedback and differing technical perspectives;
- focusing criticism on ideas and work, not people;
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them; and
- respecting project scope, privacy, and community guidelines.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination,
intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another
person's private information without permission, and unwelcome sexual
attention.

## Responsibilities and reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may take proportionate action in response to behavior they
consider unacceptable. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, or
temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

To report a concern, contact the repository owner or a project maintainer
privately through a communication channel they have provided. Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue. No reporting email address is specified
in this document; the project owner should publish a verified reporting route
before broad community participation.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers should
consider the safety and privacy of everyone involved and avoid retaliation
against people who raise concerns in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies within project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public spaces.
