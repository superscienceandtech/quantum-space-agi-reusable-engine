# License decision

## Decision

**Selected direction: MIT.** This records the project's preferred license, not
the grant of a license. No `LICENSE` file or license headers have been added.
Do not treat this repository as licensed under MIT until the project owner
authorizes and adds the license text.

## Implications

MIT is a permissive, short-form license. Once formally applied, it generally
allows use, modification, distribution, sublicensing, and sale, subject to
including the copyright and permission notice. It provides no express patent
grant and disclaims warranties and liability to the extent allowed by law.

For comparison:

- **Apache-2.0** is also permissive and includes an express patent license and
  patent-termination provisions, but has more detailed conditions.
- **GPL-family licenses** use copyleft: distribution of covered derivative
  works generally requires source availability under the applicable license
  terms. The obligations depend on the specific GPL version and use.

These are high-level summaries, not legal advice. Review the complete license
text and any contribution or third-party dependency obligations before
publication.

## Outstanding action

Before distributing code for reuse, the project owner must authorize adding the
MIT license text and confirm the appropriate copyright holder and year. Until
then, the default copyright rules may apply; the MIT direction alone does not
grant reuse rights.
